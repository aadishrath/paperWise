# Example product handbook

This sample document is included so PaperWise can be tried immediately after setup.

## Returns

Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in their original packaging. Final-sale items and gift cards cannot be returned. A refund is issued to the original payment method after the return is inspected; allow five to seven business days for the refund to appear.

## Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes three to five business days. Orders over $50 ship free. Expedited shipping is available at checkout and usually arrives within two business days.

## Support

Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific Time. Include the order number when contacting support so the team can find the purchase quickly.
